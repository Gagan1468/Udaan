Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve answered the most common ones below.

What is Udaan?

Udaan is a youth-focused financial platform that helps you understand money, investing, and entrepreneurship in a simple and practical way.

Is Udaan free to use?

Yes, most of our learning content and tools are free. Some advanced features may be added in the future.

Who can use Udaan?

Students, beginners, young professionals, and anyone who wants to improve their financial knowledge.

Do I need prior finance knowledge?

Not at all. Udaan is built for beginners and grows with you.

How can I contact the Udaan team?

You can reach us anytime via email from the Contact Us section.
